What is Bullying?

Bullying is repeated and intentional harm directed at another person, often involving a power imbalance.

Types of Bullying

  1. Emotional bullying
  2. Sexual bullying
  3. Physical bullying
  4. Verbal bullying
  5. Social bullying

We are specifically talking about sexual and emotional bullying.

What is Sexual Abuse/Bullying?

Sexual bullying is a form of bullying that involves using sexuality or gender as a weapon to intimidate, degrade, or harm someone. It includes unwanted sexual comments or touching, spreading sexual rumors, sending inappropriate pictures, or pressuring someone for sexual activity. This can happen in person, online, or over the phone — and it causes serious emotional distress.

How to Prevent Sexual Abuse

  • Avoid hazardous situations (it’s never your fault regardless of the circumstances).
  • Communicate your limits clearly.
  • Be assertive and confident.
  • Trust your instincts.
  • Respond physically if needed to get away and seek help immediately.

What is Emotional Abuse/Bullying?

Emotional bullying involves actions meant to hurt someone’s feelings, self-esteem, or mental well-being. It includes teasing, isolating someone from friends, spreading lies, and manipulating emotions to cause pain. This kind of bullying can be just as harmful as physical bullying.
